Core Engineering

Architectural Moat & Protocols

Engineered for extreme reliability in harsh physical acoustic environments.

🛡️

Hardware Enclave Bounding

Offline private signing keys reside in the smartphone's hardware Secure Enclave / StrongBox. Enforces strict monotonic nonces that cannot be rolled back or cloned even on rooted devices.

⚡

A-CSMA Carrier Sniff (15ms)

A 15ms acoustic listen-before-talk window inspects local frequency bands. Automatically activates randomized exponential backoff if another payer is currently transmitting.

🏃

Doppler Frequency Tracking

Dynamic frequency phase lock compensating pedestrian approach velocity (κ = 1 ± v/c). Ensures flawless symbol demodulation even as commuters walk rapidly toward metro turnstiles.

🔒

Receiver Auto-Disarm Loop

Receiver sampling immediately terminates within 5 milliseconds of decoding a valid cryptographic voucher, making duplicate acoustic double-charges physically impossible.

📏

Acoustic Time-of-Flight Bounding

Ultrasonic and physical acoustic attenuation bounds transactions strictly to < 1.0 meter. Eliminates rogue relay attacks and cross-lane turnstile confusion.

🔄

150ms Acoustic A-ACK Handshake

The merchant terminal emits a signed acoustic acknowledgment tone on a discrete guard channel, confirming local ledger credit and releasing payer funds from in-flight escrow.
